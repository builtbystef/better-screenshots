# Changelog

## Unreleased

First public release.

- Bring a screenshot in by file picker, drag and drop, or paste.
- Backgrounds: eight solids, six gradients, any hex colour, or your own image
  (stored in the browser).
- Nine frame presets named for where they go, padding, scale, and
  drag-to-position placement.
- Browser window wrap with a light or dark bar and your own URL.
- Effects: drop shadow, border, and rounded corners.
- PNG export at 2x, drawn by the same code as the live preview.
- Settings persist in the browser and are restored on the next visit.
